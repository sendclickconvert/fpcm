This is the single most important lesson from today, and it's in your fleet kit under "fossil middleware / prerender gap": on hybrid sites, headers belong in the **`server.mjs`/Express layer**, which wraps *every* response — static and dynamic — not in Astro middleware, which skips prerendered pages.
