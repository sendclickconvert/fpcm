Friends and Family, 
There are times in every Christian journey when things just naturally seem to go wrong — one wrong step and we sprain our ankle, one wrong turn and we end up in a wreck, and into every household, a little drama finds its way. But this note isn't about dealing with life's speed bumps, or even the worst days of our lives, but rather celebrating the Living God of Israel in our very lives. 
I led this newsletter with God's Word to us through the Prophet Zephaniah because it brings a great celebration to my heart. The Prophet's words uplift my soul, and I am reminded, even when the walls are tumbling down, that God has our back and we "shall fear disaster no more." My message is simple: life is much better with God on our side, with Christ's grace in our lives, and the power of the Holy Spirit indwelling within us. A Godly life circles around the love of God, and that love emanates to all those around us. The flip side to a Christ-centered life is drama, conflict, ego, and an obsession with money, power, or vice. So we must ask ourselves: do we want a life of Godly love and hope, or a life striving to fill the God-void with earthly distractions? 
The answer is simple: of course we want that saintly halo. But we also must be humble enough to accept that we are not perfect, our neighbor is not perfect, and our systems are equally imperfect. So we must love each other, flaws and all. Hold back judgment unless we can offer positive feedback and a helping hand. 
"They will know we are Christians by our love, by our love." 
Peace in Christ, 
Rev. Neill D. McKay

FOOD PANTRY    

   THANK YOU again to everyone who volunteered at the Pantry in July!  Our church will be on staff again in November.  During a typical month, we serve more than 300 households – fully a 100% increase over several years ago.  After a huge spike in usage, we have not seen the expected decrease since the COVID pandemic subsided. Local food drives and generous cash donations have helped limit what the Pantry has had to buy.  (Fortunately, supply chain issues have improved since the pandemic but the nonperishable items we can always use include canned fruit, veggies, tuna & pork & beans, cereal, pasta & sauce, boxed mac & cheese and boxed instant potatoes.)  When the Pantry does purchase food, it is generally from the Regional Food Bank here in Montgomery, where your donated dollars go farther than at retail supermarkets and other food suppliers.  All donations are greatly appreciated, and volunteers are always needed!  We’re open to serve clients on Tuesdays 10am-12pm and Wednesdays 6:30-8pm.  We also pack nonperishable bags on Monday and Tuesday evenings from 6-7pm so that bags are ready to go for serving clients the next day. 
If you can help out – even if it’s only a shift here and there – or if you have questions/comments/concerns, please call/text/email Robin McPhillips: 845-457-4194/845-978-6457 rmcphil1120@gmail.com.  THANK YOU!
